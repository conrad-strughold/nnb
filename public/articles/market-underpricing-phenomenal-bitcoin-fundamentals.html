In this high-energy episode of Swan Signal Live, the team dives into an action-packed week of Bitcoin and macro news. Steven kicks things off celebrating a hard-fought victory in Bitcoin Bugle’s “March Maxi Madness,” but the real meat of the show comes with analysis of the Fed’s dovish tone, Trump’s tariff plans, and growing signals of U.S. Bitcoin accumulation. Key Topics Covered: Fed Meeting Recap: John Haar lays out the Fed’s latest economic projections, highlighting Powell’s repeated emphasis on “uncertainty.” Despite fears of recession, the Fed held steady, signaling potential cuts while factoring in retaliatory tariffs. Tariff Strategy & Trump’s Playbook: Steven and Brady explore Trump’s “Liberation Day” narrative, arguing that tariffs are part of a broader negotiation strategy. Steven suggests markets may have overreacted—and that April 2 could bring upside surprises. Bitcoin’s Unshaken Fundamentals: Despite market choppiness, the Bitcoin network is stronger than ever—rising M2, resilient hash rate, and clear bullish signals from Washington, including Bo Hines’ statement: “We’re going to acquire as much Bitcoin as we can.” Why Isn’t Price Reflecting Fundamentals? The crew breaks down the disconnect: low institutional belief in government Bitcoin strategy, short-term correlation with traditional markets, and investor misunderstanding of BTC’s long-term value.
