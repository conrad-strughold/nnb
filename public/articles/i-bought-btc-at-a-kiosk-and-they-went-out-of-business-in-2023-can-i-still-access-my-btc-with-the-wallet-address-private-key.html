I bought BTC at a kiosk and they went out of business in 2023! Can I still access my BTC with the wallet address & private key?

Question

I bought bitcoin in 2022 at a BitcoinOfAmerica kiosk atm and I didnt end up doing anything more with it until now, I was going to transfer the bitcoin to my cash app btc wallet but I cant figure out how to access the wallet because bitcoin of America went out of business in 2023 so their website and customer service numbers arent any good anymore. The receipt papers I have show the transaction ID, Public wallet address and Private Key. I entered the Public Address in Google search and it came up with the amount of btc and date it was last accessed which was 2022 but gave no options to access wallet or any options at all, just that information. So then I google searched the Private Key and nothing came up. Can I still access that bitcoin???

Answer 1

Since you still have the private key and the public wallet address, your Bitcoin is likely still retrievable—but you'll need to import that private key into a secure wallet that supports manual key import. Have you tried using any trusted wallet apps or desktop wallets that allow direct private key input to regain access?
