How Bitcoin Turns Trash Into Digital Gold | Daniel Batten

(A)

“We looked at all the other solutions and Bitcoin mining was the only one that made sense.” Daniel Batten joins me to explain how Bitcoin mining can make cleaning up pollution profitable. With his first landfill project in Mexico moving forward, we get into how capturing methane can turn an environmental problem into cheap power, Bitcoin and a new source of income. We discuss the economics of one cent electricity, the upfront costs of getting these sites running, and why carbon credits are essential to making the model work. We also explore why these projects suit Bitcoin mining better than AI data centres, how they could decentralise the network, and what it would take to make Bitcoin carbon negative. We finish with Greenpeace’s campaign against Bitcoin, Troy Cross’s conversations with the artist behind the Skull of Satoshi, and how Bitcoiners challenged the environmental narrative.
