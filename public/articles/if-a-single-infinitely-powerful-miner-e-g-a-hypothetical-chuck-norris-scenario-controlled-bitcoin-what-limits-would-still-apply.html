If a single “infinitely powerful” miner (e.g., a hypothetical Chuck Norris scenario) controlled Bitcoin, what limits would still apply?

Question

Assume a purely hypothetical miner with effectively unlimited hash rate (often jokingly compared to “Chuck Norris” levels of power).

In that scenario: • Would consensus rules (like block validity) still constrain this miner? • Could they create invalid transactions or bypass signature checks? • Are there aspects of Bitcoin’s design that remain trustless even under total hash rate control?

I’m interested in the technical boundaries here, not just the usual 51% attack explanation.

Answer 1

Such miner CAN NOT violate ANY consensus rule, but he can add his own rules by refusing to mine on top of blocks that violate the additional rules, thus censoring the network.

However, this is assuming everyone else just sits and lets it happen. Others can add more hashopower and dilute the adversary. Or, they could introduce some weak subjectivity on top of PoW: https://blog.ethereum.org/2014/11/25/proof-stake-learned-love-weak-subjectivity

Ultimately, all blockchain networks are secured by their economies. People who are part of those economies will not just sit and wait while their beloved network is under censorship attack.
