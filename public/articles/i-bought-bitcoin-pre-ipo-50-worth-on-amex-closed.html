Answer 1

I bought Bitcoin pre IPO $50 worth on amex

The Initial Public offering (IPO) of Amex was in May 1977 - before Bitcoin was invented.

The Bitcoin cryptocurrency is not a corporation and so cannot have an IPO

There are hundreds of different unrelated businesses whose name includes the word bitcoin. Even in just the UK let alone the USA and worldwide.

Consequently it is hard to understand what date you mean by pre-IPO.

How much would it be now?

If you want to measure the current value of some amount of one currency (BTC) in terms of a second currency (USD), you use the exchange rates applicable on the purchase date and today.

I still need my own address.

I guess you mean a Bitcoin address.

The wording of your question suggests the electronic cash you purchased was not stored in a self-custodial wallet you created yourself on your own PC/phone/etc but was left in the hands of the business from which you purchased it. If so it may not exist other than as a debt owed you by some business. The amount you purchased may not be associated with any single Bitcoin address.

One way to get the money into an address you control yourself directly would be to download a normal (non-custodial) wallet onto your PC or phone, get that wallet to create a receiving address and ask the business who holds your bitcoin to transfer it to that address.

How is it possible for me to claim the Bitcoin I purchased?

You would have to ask the business from whom you purchased bitcoin. Only they know the answer to that.
