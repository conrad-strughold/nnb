What is the cause of this error? Is there a way to prevent this?

Answer 1

What is the cause of this error?

It is likely to be file corruption. For example due to shutting down PC before Bitcoin-Knots has finished saving data to disk.

Is there a way to prevent this?

Reindex and then take care to always wait for Bitcoin-Knots to close down fully before you shut down and turning off the PC. Keep a cycle of daily backups on multiple media and rotate some of them offsite.
