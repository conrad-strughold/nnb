Metaplanet Buys $92.5 Million Worth Of Bitcoin

Japanese Hotel firm adds 780 BTC to its treasury as Asian corporate adoption of digital assets accelerates.

Metaplanet has acquired an additional 780 Bitcoin for approximately ¥13.67 billion ($92.5 million), bringing its total holdings to 17,132 BTC as institutional Bitcoin adoption gains momentum across Asia.

According to a company filing on July 28, Metaplanet purchased the Bitcoin at an average price of ¥17.52 million ($118,622) per coin, continuing its aggressive accumulation strategy that began in April 2024. The company’s total Bitcoin holdings now stand at ¥301.7 billion ($2.03 billion).

The institutional landscape in Asia is undergoing rapid evolution. From Metaplanet’s growing 17,132 BTC treasury to new entrants like Remixpoint’s 1,168 BTC purchase, we’re seeing unprecedented corporate adoption across the region.

Metaplanet’s Bitcoin holdings have grown significantly in 2025, with the company achieving a 129.4% BTC Yield in Q2 and a 22.5% yield so far in Q3. The company uses BTC Yield as a key performance indicator to measure Bitcoin accumulation relative to share dilution.

The purchase was funded through multiple capital raises, including proceeds from the exercise of the 20th through 22nd Series of Stock Acquisition Rights.

Corporate Bitcoin treasuries are becoming a global phenomenon. With over 200 public companies worldwide now holding Bitcoin worth more than $100 billion collectively, institutional adoption continues to accelerate across markets.

At press time, Bitcoin trades near $118,800, reflecting strong market support for corporate Bitcoin treasury strategies.
